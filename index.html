ABOUT US

A fortress of flavour in Guwahati

In the Middle East and North Africa, a qasbah is a fortified citadel, a gathering place at the heart of the old town. Qasbah Lounge & Restro brings that spirit to Milan Nagar, Guwahati: a warm, welcoming lounge where authentic Middle Eastern cooking is served the way it is meant to be shared.

Our kitchen is loved for slow-cooked Mutton Mandi, Arabian-spiced Chicken Al Faham and warm, golden Kunafa, alongside tandoori favourites and North Indian classics so there is something for every table.

Whether it is a family dinner, a celebration or a relaxed evening with friends, our team looks after you with polite, attentive service from the first greeting to the last bite.
